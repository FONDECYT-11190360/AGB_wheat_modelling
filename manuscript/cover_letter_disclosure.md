# Cover-letter disclosure paragraph (draft, for the second-journal submission)

During preparation of this manuscript, the authors wish to disclose that a related work, based on the same four-site-season wheat AGB dataset, is currently under review at IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters ("Multi-Sensor Fusion for Wheat Above-Ground Biomass Estimation: Sensor Contribution Analysis Under Leave-One-Site-Out Spatial Validation"). That letter reports a sequential sensor-ablation analysis with three fixed-hyperparameter algorithms, together with additional diagnostics not covered in the present manuscript, including a conditional partial-dependence analysis of the growing-degree-day by PlanetScope EVI interaction, a site-level domain-shift assessment, and an inter-model uncertainty quantification. The present manuscript addresses a different question: how much satellite predictors add to accumulated thermal time. It introduces days-after-sowing and thermal-time baselines, single-sensor and sensor plus thermal-time predictor sets, held-out permutation importance for predictor groups, and accumulated local effects. It also removes predictors outside each set before the within-fold correlation filter and excludes PlanetScope red edge predictors that were unavailable for two site-seasons. Under these choices, the Sentinel-1 contribution in the sequential ablation depends on the algorithm, which differs in part from the letter; both works agree that no sensor provides a robust improvement, and the differences are explained in Section 4.2 of the manuscript. We are happy to provide the related manuscript to the editor upon request.
