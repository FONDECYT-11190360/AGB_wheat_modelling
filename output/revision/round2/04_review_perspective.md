## Major Issues

### M1. Within-Season Sampling May Favor ∑GDD

**Evidence**: All 153 samples within-season progressions  
**Physiological Concern**:
- Primary variance is "how far into season?"
- ∑GDD tracks this by design
- Satellites may add more value for between-season comparisons (stress detection)

**Suggested Fix**: Discuss as limitation; clarify scope ("within-season tracking")

### M2. High AGB Values Not Explained

**Evidence**: Seven samples >30 t/ha (max 43.5 t/ha)  
**Concern**: Exceptional for wheat; needs agronomic context  
**Suggested Fix**: Add irrigation/fertilization status; verify measurements

### M3. Crop Physiology Literature Could Be Better Integrated

**Gap**: GDD-biomass relationship cited only briefly (Del Pozo 1987)  
**Missing**: McMaster & Wilhelm 1997, Porter & Gawith 1999  
**Suggested Fix**: Add physiological basis (LAI → light interception → photosynthesis)
