## Changes Made

### 1. Added New Discussion Section

**Location**: Discussion §4.3 "Sampling design and generalizability" (new section between "Algorithm and predictor effects" and "Implications for AGB monitoring")

**Content**: Comprehensive paragraph (12 sentences) addressing:

1. **Sampling design description**:
   - All 153 samples are within-season progressions (sowing → ripening)
   - Tests phenological tracking within growing season

2. **What the design doesn't test**:
   - Condition differences at same phenological stage across fields
   - Example: Two fields at 800°C·d with different AGB due to stress

3. **Why design favors ∑GDD**:
   - Within-season variance dominated by phenological progression
   - Any "how far into season" predictor performs well
   - Cumulative indices also track season progress

4. **When satellites may add more value**:
   - Comparing fields at similar phenological stages
   - Different growing conditions (water stress, nutrients, pests)
   - Between-season yield anomalies after accounting for trends

5. **Scope qualification**:
   - Finding applies specifically to within-season biomass tracking
   - Not general evidence that satellites lack value for crop monitoring

6. **Future directions**:
   - Evaluate under sampling designs that isolate condition from phenology
   - Stratify by growth stage
   - Predict spatial anomalies within narrow thermal time windows

## Key Messages Added

### 1. Scope is Clear
Throughout the manuscript, the conclusion is now qualified as applying to **within-season biomass tracking**, not crop monitoring in general.

### 2. Design Limitation Explained
The within-season sampling design **systematically favors temporal predictors** like ∑GDD because:
- Most variance comes from phenological progression
- Not from condition differences at same stage

### 3. Alternative Use Cases Identified
Satellites may add more value for:
- **Stress detection**: Same thermal time, different conditions
- **Spatial anomalies**: Within-field or between-field variability
- **Between-season prediction**: Yield anomalies after detrending

### 4. Not Anti-Satellite
The finding is **not** "satellites are useless for wheat"

The finding **is** "for tracking within-season biomass progression, ∑GDD captures most transferable signal"
