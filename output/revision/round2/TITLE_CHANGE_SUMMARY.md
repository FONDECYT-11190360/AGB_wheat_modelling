## Rationale for Change

### 1. Clarity and Directness
The new title immediately communicates the key finding without requiring the reader to read the abstract first.

### 2. Scope Qualification
"Within-season" qualifier prevents misinterpretation that satellites lack value for all crop monitoring tasks.

### 3. Alignment with Revisions
The revised manuscript extensively qualifies the scope (R4 revision). The title now matches this scoping throughout.

### 4. Journal Fit
*Computers and Electronics in Agriculture* typically publishes assertive titles that state findings or demonstrate capabilities.

### 5. Prevents Overgeneralization
- **Avoids**: "Satellite sensors don't work for wheat" (too broad)
- **States**: "Thermal time dominates for within-season tracking under spatial CV" (precise scope)
